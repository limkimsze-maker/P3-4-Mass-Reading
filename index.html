Read the Scale. Lift the Weight!

Read the weighing scale carefully and fill in the mass in kilograms and grams.

Correct answer: the weightlifter lifts successfully. Wrong answer: the lift fails.
In 2-player mode, players take turns.

What pupils practise

  • Read a 5 kg circular scale
  • Recognise that each small interval is 100 g
  • Write a mass in kg and g
  • Read values such as 2 kg 300 g, 3 kg 700 g and 4 kg 900 g
